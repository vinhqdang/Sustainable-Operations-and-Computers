\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Demand uncertainty forces every online scheduler to a ratio of order $\sqrt{\theta}$.
\item CARMA adds ghost jobs for expected arrivals to an exact min-cost-flow planner.
\item CARMA meets deadlines, is $\theta$-competitive, optimal with exact predictions.
\item Dual prices give a core-stable carbon attribution among pooled data centres.
\item On 2024 British grid data, CARMA cuts the gap to the optimum by a factor of 2-3.
\end{itemize}
