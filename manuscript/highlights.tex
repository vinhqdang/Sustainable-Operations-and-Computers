\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Without demand knowledge, no online scheduler beats a ratio of order $\sqrt{\theta}$.
\item CARMA adds ghost jobs for expected arrivals to a min-cost-flow planner.
\item Under a backstop, CARMA is deadline-safe and optimal with exact predictions.
\item CARMA beats all baselines lacking demand information on five grids.
\item Dual prices give a core-stable attribution; origin/host accounting is not stable.
\end{itemize}
