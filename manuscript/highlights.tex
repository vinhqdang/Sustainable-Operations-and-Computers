\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Without demand knowledge, no online scheduler beats a ratio of order $\sqrt{\theta}$.
\item CARMA adds ghost jobs for expected arrivals to an exact min-cost-flow planner.
\item With a backstop, CARMA meets all deadlines; it is optimal with exact predictions.
\item On British grid data and an Alibaba trace, CARMA cuts myopic gaps by 43--60\%.
\item Dual prices give a core-stable carbon attribution; origin-based charging does not.
\end{itemize}
